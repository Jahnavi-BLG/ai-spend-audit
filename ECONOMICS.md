# Startup Economics & Projections

## Concept
The AI Spend Audit Tool is currently a free lead-generation tool. The overarching business model is to use the free audit to capture highly qualified leads (companies with high software spend) and either upsell them to a premium SaaS management platform or sell the leads to B2B SaaS optimization consultants.

## Estimated Lead Value
- If a company is spending >$1,000/mo on AI tools, they are a high-value target.
- **Lead Value:** A qualified B2B email (Manager/Founder) with attached intent data (we know their exact tech stack) is conservatively worth **$15 - $25** to a B2B consulting firm or SaaS management platform like Vendr or Tropic.

## CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) Assumptions
Because our initial GTM strategy relies on organic Reddit/Twitter posting and Product Hunt (content-led growth), our initial CAC is effectively **$0** (just sweat equity).
If we scale to paid ads (e.g., LinkedIn Ads targeting Engineering Managers):
- Estimated Cost Per Click (CPC): $4.00
- Landing Page Conversion Rate: 15%
- **Estimated Blended CAC:** ~$26 per captured lead.

## Conversion Funnel Math (Monthly Estimate)
Assuming organic traction from a successful Product Hunt launch and a few viral tweets:
- **Top of Funnel (Website Visitors):** 5,000 visitors
- **Mid Funnel (Ran an Audit):** 1,500 users (30% conversion rate)
- **Bottom Funnel (Captured Email via Lead Form):** 150 leads (10% of those who ran an audit)

## Rough Revenue Projections
**Model A: Lead Generation (Selling Data)**
- 150 highly qualified leads per month.
- Sold to a partner agency at $20/lead.
- **Monthly Revenue:** $3,000 / month.

**Model B: "Pro" Tier SaaS Upsell**
Instead of selling leads, we offer a "Pro" tier for $19/mo that integrates directly with company credit cards to automatically track AI spend every month.
- 150 leads captured.
- 5% convert to paid SaaS tier (7.5 new customers/mo).
- Month 1 MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue): $142.50
- Month 12 MRR (assuming steady growth and low churn): ~$1,700/mo.

## Realistic Assumptions
- The 30% visitor-to-audit rate is realistic because the tool is interactive and doesn't require an email upfront.
- The 10% email capture rate assumes the lead capture form is placed *after* they see the value of the audit, perhaps to "unlock" the full PDF report or get customized consulting.
- The real challenge isn't the math, it's maintaining the 5,000 monthly visitors organically once the initial launch hype dies down. SEO for terms like "AI tool cost calculator" will be critical.
