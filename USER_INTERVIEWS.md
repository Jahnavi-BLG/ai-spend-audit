# USER_INTERVIEWS.md

## Interview 1 — CSE Graduate

Role:
Works as Software Developer.

Does your company use AI? If yes, what AI tools?
"Yes, we use AI almost daily in our work. We are provided with Claude Pro subscription."

How much does your company spend on AI tools?
"They don't disclose that to us."

Would your company or you use this AI spend audit tool?
"Most definitely, it gets hard to research and buy an accurate plan so a tool which says how much to spend within few steps would be great."

Most surprising thing:
The employee mentioned that they were not aware of how much the company actually spends on AI subscriptions, even though they use AI tools daily.

What it changed about my design:
This made me prioritize simplicity and visibility of cost breakdowns in the audit results page.

## Interview 2 — CSE Student

Role:
Does college projects.

Do you use AI to develop your projects? If yes, what AI tools?
"Yes, to make projects for my college submissions. I use Claude, ChatGPT and Anti Gravity mostly."

Did you buy any subscriptions of those AI tools? If yes, how much do you spend on AI tools?
"No, I just use the free version."

Would you use this AI Spend Audit Tool now or in future?
"Yes, would consult one before purchasing any in future."

Most surprising thing:
The student relied heavily on free AI plans and had never compared pricing or subscription options before.

What it changed about my design:
This encouraged me to make the tool beginner-friendly and useful even for small teams or students exploring paid AI tools.
